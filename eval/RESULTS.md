# BrandProof — evaluation results (honest history)

All numbers can be reproduced with the commands shown. Test sets were written by the team with AI assistance
(they are **not** written by independent people — we committed to "independently written" cases in Round 2 and
could not arrange outside writers in the 72-hour window; see "What we did not deliver" in the final PDF).

## 1. Text rule engine — `npm run eval`

| Stage | Cases | Precision | Recall | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Round 2 set (`eval/testset.json`) | 36 | 96.4% | 100% | reported in Round 2 |
| **Round 3 set, first run** (`eval/testset-r3.json` + Round 2 set) | 177 | **90.0%** | **99.2%** | 141 new cases, 69 of them clean, many deliberately tricky |
| Labels corrected | — | — | — | 3 of our own labels were wrong (e.g. "BONUS rewards. T&C apply." does include T&C) |
| After engine fixes | 177 | 100% | 100% | optimistic: this set guided the fixes |
| **Holdout, first run** (`npm run eval -- --holdout`, written before running) | 30 | **91.3%** | **95.5%** | 1 miss was our label error (NovaSave is the approved spelling) → 91.3% / 100% |
| After fixing the 2 holdout false positives | 30 | 100% | 100% | no longer unseen |

**What the Round 3 set taught us (fixes made to the engine):**
- brand names inside URLs and email addresses (`paynova.in/help`) are no longer flagged as misspellings;
- negated guarantees ("returns are *not* guaranteed") are treated as compliant disclaimers;
- "Best regards" and "leading to" are not superlative claims;
- "we offer" / "our app offers" (verb) is not a promotional offer that needs T&C;
- all-caps brand names (CRED, BHIM) and common acronyms (DTH, LPG, KYC, IFSC…) are not "shouting";
- a word already flagged by another rule is not double-flagged as capitals.

The number we stand behind for unseen text is the **holdout first run: 91.3% precision, 95.5% recall** (30 cases).

## 2. Visual Compliance Engine — `npm run eval:visual`

30 labelled creatives in `eval/creatives` (generated by `eval/make_creatives.py`, labels in `labels.json`):
clean (5), retired logo (4), stretched logo (4), recoloured logo (4), low-contrast text (4), small text (3),
no logo (2), two-colour creatives with a coloured footer band (4 — 2 clean, 2 low-contrast).

| Issue | TP | FP | FN | Precision | Recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retired logo (V-04) | 4 | 0 | 0 | 100% | 100% |
| Stretched logo (V-05) | 4 | 0 | 0 | 100% | 100% |
| Recoloured logo (V-06) | 4 | 0 | 0 | 100% | 100% |
| Missing logo (V-07) | 2 | 0 | 0 | 100% | 100% |
| Low text contrast (V-03) | 5 | 0 | 1 | 100% | 83% |
| Text too small (V-08) | 3 | 0 | 0 | 100% | 100% |
| **All** | 22 | 0 | 1 | **100%** | **95.7%** |

29 of 30 creatives fully correct. The miss: text at about 1.4:1 contrast is so faint that OCR cannot read it at all, so
it cannot be measured. Two development iterations changed the numbers: edge-only logo matching locked onto bold
headlines (5/26 correct), fixed by combining intensity and edge correlation; OCR skipped text on coloured footer
bands, fixed by reading each background band separately. Caveat: the creatives are synthetic and made by us.

## 4. Self-improving Brand Kit — `npm run test:rules`
On the demo history, rule T-04 ("no pressure tactics") was overridden in 75% of decisions. The keyword draft
(exception: "a real end date or deadline is stated") re-run on every past version removes all 3 pointless flags,
misses 0 issues people agreed were real, and drops the override rate from 75% to 0%. The AI draft (Gemini) scored
75% → 50% because it scoped the exception to the same sentence; the product tests both drafts and pre-selects the better one.
